Codebase City viewer: palette options

Each option shows a sequential ramp (cold to hot, used by the hotspot, churn, age and bug-fix modes), the shared Okabe-Ito categorical palette (language and ownership modes, 8 authors plus grey for "others"), the neutral grey for cold files, and a small in-context sample, in dark and light. All ramps are perceptually ordered by lightness so they survive common colour-vision deficiencies; the legend always carries numbers, never colour alone.
